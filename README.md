# D2 Roll Check

Score any Destiny 2 weapon roll from 0 to 100 against the rolls the community recommends.

**Use it here: https://d2rollcheck.com**

## What it does

- **Score a roll**: search a weapon, tap the perks on your drop, and get a score and grade. The page
  shows which perks pair well together, the curator's notes on the closest recommended roll, the
  weapon's stats with your perks applied, and how the gun's usage has changed over time.
- **My inventory**: sign in with Bungie to score every weapon you own at once. Sort by score or by
  newest, find duplicates and shard candidates, see what's waiting at the Postmaster, and switch a
  weapon to its best perks without opening the game.
- **Xûr**: the weapons Xûr is selling this weekend, scored.
- **Compare**: put up to three guns side by side, and see every gun of a type ranked by how often
  players actually use it.

Tap the ☆ next to any weapon to save it as a favorite. Favorites are kept in your browser.

## How the score works

A roll score compares your roll with other rolls of the **same gun**:

- **Closeness (55%)**: how many perks you share with the nearest recommended roll.
- **Perk strength (45%)**: how often each of your perks appears in recommended rolls that go with
  your other picks. Trait perks count the most.
- **Real usage**: once a gun has been seen at least 30 times in sampled matches, perks that players
  actually run can raise the score. Usage never lowers the score of a gun that has curator rolls.

Grades: 90+ God roll, 75+ Keeper, 55+ Solid, 35+ Situational, below that Shard it.

A high score on a weak gun is still a weak gun. The **gun rank** on each weapon and the Compare tab
show how the gun itself stands among guns of its type.

## Where the data comes from

- **Recommended rolls and notes**: the
  [DIM community wish list](https://github.com/48klocs/dim-wish-list-sources), compiled from many
  community curators. Their notes are shown with their names as written.
- **Weapons, perks, stats and icons**: the Bungie API.
- **Usage**: a daily sample of public match reports. It shows what players use, which isn't always
  what's strongest. New, easy-to-farm and craftable guns show up more.

Data refreshes once a day.

## Sign-in and privacy

- Signing in is optional. Scoring a roll, Compare and the rankings work without it.
- Sign-in happens on Bungie's own site. D2 Roll Check never sees your password.
- Your inventory is read by your own browser, directly from Bungie. This site has no server and
  stores nothing about you. Nobody else, including the site's owner, can see your inventory.
- The site only changes something on your account when you press an **Apply** button, and then it
  only switches a weapon between perks it already has. It never moves or deletes items.
- Bungie signs you out of the site after about an hour.

## Good to know

- Scores reflect community picks. Some top rolls are built for a specific subclass or playstyle, so
  read the notes before you delete anything.
- Guns with no recommended rolls yet are scored from similar weapons and marked "Estimated".
- Stats leave out masterworks, mods and situational bonuses.
