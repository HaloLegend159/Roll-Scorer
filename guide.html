What is a god roll in Destiny 2?

A god roll is a copy of a weapon with the best possible perks for what you want to do with it. Most legendary weapons drop with random perks: a barrel, a magazine and two trait columns, and sometimes more than one option per column. With dozens of perks per column, most drops are average, a few are great, and one or two combinations are what players chase. Those are the god rolls.

There isn't one god roll per gun. The best perks for clearing enemies in a raid are rarely the best for winning duels in the Crucible, and a perk that's perfect with one subclass can be useless with another.

PvE rolls vs PvP rolls

PvE rolls usually favour damage boosts, perks that clear groups of enemies, and perks that keep ammo, reloads or abilities flowing through long activities. PvP rolls usually favour range, stability and handling, plus perks that help you win the first fight and the one right after. A roll can be excellent in one and only fine in the other, so D2 Roll Check scores both separately. Pick PvE or PvP above any weapon to switch.

How D2 Roll Check scores a roll

Every score is out of 100 and compares your perks with the rolls the Destiny 2 community recommends for that gun, using the community wish lists also used by Destiny Item Manager.

  • Closeness to a recommended roll is a little over half the score: how many of your perks match the nearest roll that curators recommend.
  • How often each perk is recommended makes up the rest, and it knows which perks pair well. A trait that's great with your other trait counts for more than one that's only great with something you don't have.
  • Traits matter most. The two trait columns count three times as much as the barrel or magazine, and origin traits count half as much.
  • Real usage can lift a score. When enough copies of a gun show up in sampled matches, perks players actually use can raise the score. They never lower it, so an older roll that's still strong but out of fashion isn't punished.

For guns that no curator has posted rolls for yet, the score is estimated from recommended rolls on similar weapons with the same frame, and is marked as estimated.

What the grades mean

God roll
100. Matches a top recommended roll exactly. Keep it.

90 to 99. One small step away from a god roll.
Keeper
75 to 89. A strong roll worth using.

55 to 74. Good enough to use until something better drops.
Situational
35 to 54. Has something useful, but only for certain builds or activities.
Dismantle
Below 35. Few or none of the perks people look for on this gun.

Should I dismantle this roll?

Before you dismantle anything, check three things. First, the curator notes on the weapon: some top rolls are built for one subclass or playstyle, and a perk that scores low might be exactly what your build needs. Second, whether your copy has a second perk option in any column, because the best combination may already be on the gun. Third, whether it's craftable, because Deepsight copies help unlock the pattern even if the roll is bad. The My inventory page does the second check for you on every weapon you own and flags what's safe to dismantle.

What do the gold dots mean?

When you pick a weapon, a solid gold dot marks the most recommended perk in each column and a gold ring marks a strong alternative. After you pick a perk, the dots in the other columns update to show what pairs best with it.

Where the data comes from

Weapons, perks and stats come from Bungie's official API. Recommended rolls come from the DIM community wish list. Usage comes from sampling weapons used in recent matches. Everything updates once a day.
